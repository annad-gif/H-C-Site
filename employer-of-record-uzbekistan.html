What an Employer of Record is

Your legal employer in Uzbekistan

An Employer of Record (EOR) is a company that legally employs people on behalf of another business. Human and Capital becomes the official employer of your team members in Uzbekistan: we sign employment contracts under Uzbek labour law, run payroll, pay taxes and keep HR records. The employees work full-time on your tasks, under your management.

For a foreign company this is the fastest way to start hiring in Uzbekistan. Setting up a local legal entity takes months, an accountant, a lawyer and a payroll provider. With an EOR you get one partner, one contract and one invoice – and you can have an employee at work in a few weeks.

Unlike most EOR platforms, we are a Tashkent-based recruitment agency: we also find the people. Recruitment, employment and day-to-day HR support come from one local team. Read more in our guide Hiring in Uzbekistan.

FAQ

Employer of Record in Uzbekistan: FAQ

Do I need a legal entity in Uzbekistan to hire employees?

No. With an Employer of Record we are the legal employer. Your employees get official contracts under Uzbek labour law, and you manage their work without registering a company in Uzbekistan.

Is using an EOR legal in Uzbekistan?

Yes. The employees are officially employed by our company in Uzbekistan, with payroll, taxes and reporting handled in line with local law.

How fast can I hire through an EOR?

For most roles a new employee can start within 2–4 weeks, including recruitment. Rare or senior roles may take longer; we agree on the timeline before the search starts.

Who manages the employee's daily work?

You do. The employee works on your tasks, follows your processes and reports to your managers. We handle employment, payroll, taxes and HR administration.

What is the difference between EOR and outstaffing?

They are very close. In both models we are the legal employer and you direct the work. Our EOR service also includes recruitment, so you get people, employment and payroll in one contract. See also our outstaffing page.

Can you hire employees who already work for us as contractors?

Yes. We can take your existing contractors in Uzbekistan into official employment, which removes the misclassification risk.

How is the cost of EOR calculated?

The cost is calculated individually and depends on the number of employees, the roles and the scope of services. Send us a request and we will prepare a proposal within one business day.
