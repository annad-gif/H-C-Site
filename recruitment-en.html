FAQ

Recruitment in Uzbekistan: frequently asked questions

How long does recruitment take?

It depends on the role, the labour market and the conditions the employer offers. We agree on the timeline before the search starts and keep you updated.

How much does recruitment cost?

The cost is calculated individually and depends on the level of the role, the timeline and the complexity of the search. We prepare an exact quote after a brief on the vacancy.

What guarantees do you give?

One free replacement within the agreed terms, a free trial day for the candidate, and a refund of the prepayment if we do not present at least three suitable candidates within the agreed timeframe. We also support the employee and the employer during probation.

Do you search for top managers?

Yes. For executives and rare specialists we use direct search, because such candidates rarely respond to job ads.

Do you handle mass recruitment?

Yes, we fill dozens of similar roles in tight timelines for retail, BPO and contact centres, and manufacturing.

Can I hire a candidate from your CV database?

Yes. You choose a candidate from our CV database, and the agency receives a fixed fee only if the candidate starts work.

Do you work with international companies?

Yes. We recruit for local and international companies, and if you need to employ a team in Uzbekistan without your own legal entity, we offer outstaffing.

Do you use AI in recruitment?

Yes. Modern tools help us analyse CVs faster, while experienced recruiters make every decision about candidates.
